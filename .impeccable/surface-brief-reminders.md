# Surface brief — /reminders (hero), with /, /review, /ledger

Mode: **Operate**. Phone-first, 390px baseline. Audience: a neighbourhood shop
owner, alone at closing time, on one shared Android phone.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The reminder screen is a catalogue entry, not a dashboard. It
refuses the arrangement this category always ships — stat cards above a table,
blue primary button below — because the shopkeeper's real question is never
"how much is owed in total". It is "who do I ask tonight, and what do I say to
them". One idea owns the page: the figure is the headline, and the name is
decoded, not displayed.

**OWN-WORLD.** Matte black (#0b0b0c), no cards, no shadows, no elevation. All
structure is 1px hairlines (#3a3a3c). Near-white ink (#f2f2ef) for figures and
data; engraved-feel tracked caps (Rajdhani, +0.14em) for labels only. Every
figure is tabular mono (Spline Sans Mono) on one shared baseline at one fixed
scale. Identity is a 6px hue strip — never a name — until it is decoded.
Direction is never colour alone: a filled slab means credit given, a hollow
slab with a struck rule means money received, so the page survives greyscale
and printing. Remove all content and it is still recognisable: black field,
hairline ruling, one white plot, four columns.

**STORY.** Understand: this is the book, in strict page order. Believe: the
figure is certain because a human confirmed it, and a name is not exposed to
whoever is standing at the counter. Do: decode the top entry, read the message
in the customer's own language, copy it into WhatsApp — one tap per customer.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** 390×844. A 56px hairline header: shop name in tracked caps
at 11px, left; three language keys as small square controls, right. Below, the
plot band, 120px: one white hairline per customer, the selected line at double
weight, a crossing tick where a customer both owes and has paid. Beneath it,
the total outstanding right-aligned on the same right edge as every figure on
the page, in the page's largest type. Then the sequence: 68px rows of
[page code] [hue strip] [amount], hairline-ruled, first row pre-decoded on load
so the mechanism is visible without a tap. Its message sits under it in Mukta
at reading size, with the single Copy action full-width in the bottom third,
inside thumb reach.

**FORM.** The dealer's first card, *The Catalogue* — a Factory-era catalog
sleeve: matte black, one white hairline plot owning the field, hue strips
standing in for names, state spoken in code, decoded on touch. Seed key
`d2007df6`, dealt under the user-requested bolder register.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
